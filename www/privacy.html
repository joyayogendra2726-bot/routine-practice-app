Privacy Policy

TileStreak — Daily Habit & Streak Grid Tracker • Effective Date: November 15, 2026

1. Overview

TileStreak respects your privacy. This application is designed to work locally on your device without requiring an account, login, or cloud registration.

2. Data Collection & Storage

We do not collect, store, or transmit your personal habit entries, names, emails, or usage logs to external servers. All habit names, check-ins, and streak data are stored exclusively in local storage on your device. You can export or delete your data at any time using the in-app controls.

3. Advertising & Third-Party Services

If future versions of TileStreak display advertisements (such as Google AdMob) or offer optional in-app purchases via Google Play Billing, those third-party providers may process standard device identifiers in accordance with their own privacy policies and Google Play guidelines.

4. Children's Privacy

TileStreak is a general productivity tool intended for users aged 18 and above and is not directed at children under 13.

5. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please reach out using the developer support email listed on the TileStreak Google Play Store page.
